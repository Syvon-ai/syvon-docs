# Credits

Credits are the unit of work in Syvon. Anything that costs real compute —
generating content, rendering video, synthesizing voice, transcribing audio —
spends credits. Everything else (chatting, editing text, organizing,
publishing) is free.

## What spends, roughly

| Action | Cost shape |
|---|---|
| **Generating** a piece (planning + designing) | Low — iterate freely |
| **Editing text** on an existing piece | Free |
| **Rendering** video | The main spend — the heavier the piece, the more |
| **Generating media** (images, video clips) | Spends per generation |
| **Voice** (narration, transcription) | Spends per piece |

Your balance and history are in your workspace settings.

## The iteration economy

The whole design pushes cheap iteration *before* expensive commitment:

1. **Chat and preview cheaply.** Copy fixes, shot changes, and layout nudges
   before render cost little or nothing.
2. **Render once, at the end.** The preview is faithful — what you scrub is
   what renders. If the preview is right, render.
3. **Regenerate rather than re-render endlessly.** If a piece needs a new
   idea, a fresh generation is often cheaper than a stack of corrections.

## When something fails

You are not charged for work that failed — a crashed render or a rejected
generation doesn't consume credits. If a spend ever looks wrong, your credit
history shows each event, and that's the record to point at.
