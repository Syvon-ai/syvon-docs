# Brands

A **brand** is Syvon's memory of how you look and sound. Colors, fonts, logo,
imagery style, and voice live in the brand — and every piece your agents make
inherits them automatically. You set the brand once; you almost never touch
it per piece.

## The pieces of DNA

| Element | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Colors** | Palette + each color's role; every layout maps its parts to roles |
| **Typography** | Heading, body, and mono families, plus the type scale |
| **Logo** | The marks and their variants — full lockup, symbol, reversed |
| **Imagery** | The photo/illustration style generation follows |
| **Voice** | How the copy sounds — terse, warm, technical — across every generation |

## Two ways to get one

1. **Import** — give Syvon your website and it extracts the colors, fonts,
   and logo into a brand you then adjust.
2. **Propose** — describe the brand you want ("bold, dark, editorial fintech")
   and Syvon drafts a complete DNA for you to accept or tweak.

Both are one command in a chat; ask your agent to set up your brand and it
will walk the choice with you.

## Brands are shared, agents are scoped

A workspace can hold several brands, and any agent can be given any of them.
Common setups:

- **One brand, several agents** — the usual case: same company, different
  content jobs.
- **Several brands, one workspace** — sub-brands or a client roster; each
  agent is pinned to one brand.
- **One brand per workspace** — when separation matters more than convenience.

## Change the brand, restyle the future

Editing brand DNA changes what *future* generations produce. Existing pieces
keep what they were made with — regenerate them if you want them in the new
look. That is also why iterating on brand early is the highest-leverage thing
you can do.
