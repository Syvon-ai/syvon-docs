# Your first content

This is the whole product in one run. You will write a brief, watch Syvon plan
and design the piece, then render it as a video. Thirty minutes at most.

## Before you start

- You can sign in to your workspace.
- Your workspace has at least one **agent** (most do — check your workspace
  home). If not, do [Create an agent](/learn/tutorials/create-an-agent) first; it
  takes five minutes.
- You have some credits. Generation and rendering spend them; you can see your
  balance in your workspace settings. (See [Credits](/learn/credits).)

## 1. Open the chat

In Studio, open your agent's conversation. This chat *is* the working surface —
everything else (canvas, render, publish) hangs off it.

## 2. Write a brief, not a spec

Say what the piece is **for** and **who** it is for. A good first brief:

> A 20-second launch teaser for our new adapter line. Audience: bikepackers
> who care about weight. Punchy, minimal, end on the logo.

You do not need to describe layouts, fonts, or shot lists — that is the
agent's job, and it knows your brand already.

## 3. Watch the plan come back

The agent responds with the piece it made — a storyboarded composition you can
preview right there. Don't like something? Reply the way you would to a
person:

> "Shorter on the intro, and make the headline bigger."

Each reply regenerates only what changed, so iterating is cheap.

## 4. Render it

When the preview is right, ask for a render. The composition becomes a real
video file. This is the step that takes a few minutes — the render runs
server-side, and you can keep chatting while it finishes.

## 5. Decide what happens next

With the video in hand you have three options, all covered later in
[Tutorials](/learn/tutorials/publish-to-your-feed):

- **Publish** it to your feed and site,
- **Export** the file for use anywhere else, or
- **Iterate** — a new brief on the same piece.

## You now know

- The chat is where work happens; everything else is downstream of it.
- Briefs describe outcomes; the agent handles the craft.
- Preview first, render second — iterating before render costs the least.
