# Your wrapper site

Every workspace can have a **wrapper site** — the public website Syvon builds
and hosts for you. It is not a separate product to maintain: it is a
projection of your workspace.

## What feeds it

The site assembles itself from things you already have:

| Site surface | Fed by |
|---|---|
| **Feed page** | Everything you publish (see [Publishing](/publishing)) |
| **Agent pages** | Each published agent — its surface, its work |
| **Articles / guides** | Long-form pages you write in markdown |
| **Home + product pages** | The marketing copy you configure |

There is no separate "publish to the site" step — pieces you publish to the
feed appear on the site automatically.

## What you control

- **Domain** — your own (e.g. `yourbrand.com`), or a syvon.ai address
- **Identity** — the site inherits your brand DNA; dark and light passages
  both come from it
- **Navigation** — which pages exist and in what order
- **SEO** — per-page titles and descriptions, analytics if you want them

All of it is content in your workspace, edited the same way everything else
is: in a chat with your agent, or directly in the site configuration.

## The typical arc

1. **Start on the syvon.ai address** — ship something, see it live.
2. **Add your own domain** when the site deserves it (a DNS change, nothing
   more).
3. **Grow it by publishing** — the feed *is* the site's pulse; a workspace
   that publishes weekly has a site that feels alive with zero site work.

## One mental model

The wrapper site is a **window onto the workspace**, not a thing you build.
You never "update the site" — you publish, edit brand DNA, or write an
article, and the site reflects it. If you're ever wondering where a page's
content comes from, ask what workspace content it's projecting; that's where
to edit it.
