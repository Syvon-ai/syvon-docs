# Welcome to Syvon

Syvon turns a written brief into finished, on-brand content — videos, posts,
and pages — without you opening a design tool. You describe what you want in
plain language; Syvon plans it, designs it in your brand, renders it, and
publishes it to your site and social surfaces.

You are in the right place if you want to **learn the product**. This site is
task-oriented: short concept pages, then tutorials you follow with your own
workspace open.

If you want the **API and SDK reference** instead — endpoints, authentication,
types — that lives separately at [docs.syvon.ai](https://docs.syvon.ai).

## What you will learn here

1. **How Syvon thinks** — the pipeline from brief to published content
   ([How it works](/how-it-works)).
2. **The four building blocks** — workspaces, brands, agents, and flows.
3. **The hands-on skills** — making your first video, publishing to your feed,
   putting your site live, even driving Syvon from Claude.

## How to use this site

- **New to Syvon?** Start with [Quick tour](/quick-tour), then do
  [Your first content](/first-content). Twenty minutes, end to end.
- **Have a workspace already?** Jump straight to the
  [Tutorials](/tutorials/create-an-agent) for the task in front of you.
- **Heard a term you don't know?** The [Glossary](/glossary) has it in one
  line each.

## One rule of thumb

Everything in Syvon is a *conversation*. If you can describe the outcome you
want, you are already most of the way there — and anything Syvon makes, you
can ask to change.
